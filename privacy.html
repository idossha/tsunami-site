Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Tsunami does not collect any data.

The app has no account of its own, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. The only network it uses is Apple's iCloud, and only to keep your own devices in step, as described below.

Your answers, review history, settings and imported decks are stored on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them). The developer cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them from that device.

iCloud sync. If your device is signed in to iCloud, your answers and review history are also kept in the private part of your own iCloud account, so that your iPhone and iPad show the same progress. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; the developer has no access to it. You can switch it off in Settings → iCloud sync. Reset progress removes it from iCloud and from your devices; you can also delete it in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Settings and imported decks are not synced.

When you export your progress, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or Messages). Decks you import stay on your device.

Drawings you make on sketch cards are never saved.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page before the change takes effect.

Contact: the support page

Tsunami is free: no ads, no purchases, no account, and nothing is collected.

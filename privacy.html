Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Tsunami does not collect any data.

The app makes no network requests. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that reports anything.

Your answers, review history, settings and imported decks are stored only on your iPhone (and in your device backups, if you use them). The developer cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

When you export your progress, the file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you choose to send it (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or Messages). Decks you import stay on your iPhone.

Drawings you make on sketch cards are never saved.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page before the change takes effect.

Contact: the support page

Tsunami is free: no ads, no purchases, no account, and nothing is collected.

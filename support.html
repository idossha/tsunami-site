Support

Report a wrong card or a bug

Open an issue on the project's issue page. Issues there are public, so leave out anything personal. A GitHub account is needed to open one.

For a card, give its title and the deck it is in, and say what looks wrong; the textbook section it cites, shown on the back of the card, helps. For a bug, give your device model and iOS or iPadOS version and what you did just before it happened.

Back up your progress

Your progress lives on your devices (and in their backups) and, with Settings → iCloud sync on and the device signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account, which keeps an iPhone and an iPad in step by itself. Sync is in beta and off unless you switch it on; export your progress before you do. The line under the switch says whether it is up to date, syncing, or off and why. While sync is on, resetting or restoring progress on one device does the same on all of them. Settings → Export progress saves it as a file you can keep in Files or send to yourself; Settings → Restore progress reads it back, on the same phone or a new one. Restoring replaces the progress on the phone, so the app asks first.

Your own decks

Tsunami opens deck files ending in .tsunamideck. Open one from Files, AirDrop or another app's share sheet and it appears in the Library; progress on a deck follows its deck and card ids, so importing an updated version keeps your history. A deck with an error is not imported. A deck you import is yours: it stays on your iPhone, and the checks described on this site apply to the built-in deck only.

Tsunami is free: no ads, no purchases, no account, and nothing is collected.
